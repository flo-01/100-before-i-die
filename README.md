# 100 Before I Die

A retro, 3D ASCII tracker for your list of 100 games to play before you die.

**Open it:** https://flo-01.github.io/100-before-i-die/ (or download `index.html` and open it in any browser)

- A spinning 3D ASCII cartridge over a synthwave grid. Its label fills up as you beat games.
- Track up to 100 games: BACKLOG → PLAYING → BEATEN, with ratings, filters and search.
- **Follow the 100 Lives list:** on an empty list, tap **LOAD THE 100 LIVES LIST** to load all 100 games in rank order. Or build your own.
- **Cloud Save:** sync your list across devices with a GitHub token (only the `gist` scope). It's stored in a secret gist in your own account.
- Works offline, installs to your phone's home screen, and has green, amber and cyan CRT themes.

Your list is saved in your own browser and, if you turn it on, your own GitHub account. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
